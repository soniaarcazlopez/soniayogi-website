02, hybrid

Yang & Yin

Sundays · 5:00 to 6:30pm · 90 mins
all levelsvinyasayin

A complete practice that balances effort with ease. We begin with a dynamic Vinyasa flow to build heat, strength and flexibility, syncing breath with drishti and bandhas through a Yang transition, then sink into a restorative Yin practice to soothe the nervous system. You'll leave deeply centred, nourished and renewed.

Drop-in: donation based. Package of 4 classes: £45 · Gift package of 5 classes: £55.

Yogasana

Tuesdays · 6:30 to 8:00pm · 90 mins
all levelsdynamic flowmantra

Crave the community energy of an in-person session or the convenience of practising at home? Our dual-streamed class is for you. We open with meditation and mantra, then move into a dynamic Yogasana flow that builds core strength and heat, linking breath with drishti and bandhas to release tension and build functional flexibility, with real-time alignment cues for everyone.

Morning Meditations

Weekday mornings · 7:15am UK · 20 mins
all levelslive onlinestillness

Start your day with stillness. We are training the mind to find quiet before the world gets noisy, sitting still, becoming curious about what's present, watching thoughts and feelings move through. You have thoughts, but you are not your thoughts. A short, grounding way to begin again, each day.

Once a year · annual retreat

Portugal · Terra Raíz, Alcobaça

8 to 11 October 2027 · Limited spaces, first come, first served

Pause · Breathe · Be Present

£770.00full retreat price

Secure your spot with a £200 deposit

To keep this retreat intimate, spaces at Terra Raíz are limited. Lock in your place early with a non-refundable deposit, then spread the remaining balance in instalments leading up to autumn 2027.

Deposits are non-refundable to secure room reservations with the venue, with the final balance due 60 days before the retreat (by 9 August 2027). Read the full terms.

In a world that rarely slows down, this retreat offers a sanctuary to disconnect. We focus on the "work-in," combining Vinyasa Flow to build strength with Yin and Yoga Nidra to soothe the nervous system. Whether you're hiking the Portuguese countryside or meditating at sunrise, this is your time to reclaim your peace.

  • SunriseEarly morning meditation followed by a dynamic, heat-building Yogasana practice.
  • MorningFresh, healthy breakfast shared with the group.
  • AfternoonFree time for self-reflection, hiking the Serra de Aire, or a trip to Nazaré or Batalha.
  • Late afternoonA grounding Vinyasa Flow transitioning into deep Yin or Yoga Nidra.
  • EveningA communal, nourishing dinner under the stars.

Included: accommodation and towels, breakfast and dinner, fruit, nuts and drinks, all yoga sessions and props, and a group day trip. Not included: flights, airport transfers (available on request for an extra fee) and travel insurance.
