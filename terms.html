Terms & Conditions

Welcome to soniayogi.com. By booking or taking part in any online or in-person yoga class, private session or workshop, you agree to the terms below.

Last updated October 2026. Retreat bookings have their own retreat terms.

1. Health and safety

Your health. Yoga involves physical movement. By taking part, you confirm that you are in good health and able to do so. If you have a medical condition, an injury, have had recent surgery, or are pregnant, please tell me before you book and again before class starts, so I can offer modifications. I may ask you to check with your doctor first.

Listen to your body. You practise at your own risk. Adjust or stop any movement that causes discomfort or pain, and rest whenever you need to.

Under 18s. Participants under 18 need written consent from a parent or guardian.

Liability. soniayogi is not responsible for injury or loss arising from your participation, except where caused by our negligence. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be excluded by law.

2. Online classes

Camera on. For your safety, and so I can give you alignment feedback and verbal adjustments, please keep your camera on during live online classes. If your camera stays off, you may be removed from the session without a refund.

Your space and connection. Please make sure you have a stable internet connection and enough clear space to move safely before class begins. I am not responsible for connection problems on your side.

Privacy. Recording or taking screenshots of live classes is not allowed, to respect the privacy of everyone taking part.

3. In-person classes and private sessions

Arrival. Please arrive 5 to 10 minutes before the start time to settle in. Late arrivals may not be admitted, so others can practise undisturbed.

Belongings. Please keep your personal items neatly stored. soniayogi and the venue are not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged belongings.

Etiquette. Please switch your phone to silent and help keep the space calm for everyone.

Sessions at your home or chosen venue. Please make sure the space is clear, safe and suitable for practice.

4. Bookings, cancellations and refunds

Payment. All classes must be paid for, or your donation made, before you attend.

If you cancel:

  • 24 hours or more before class: you can reschedule, or receive a credit towards a future session.
  • Less than 24 hours before class, or no-show: no refund, and the booking cannot be transferred.

To cancel, email info@soniayogi.com or cancel through your Eventbrite booking.

If I cancel. In the rare event that I cancel a class, I will let you know as early as possible and offer you a full refund or a class credit.

5. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, and continuing to attend classes after an update means you accept the new terms.

Questions about these terms? Please get in touch via the contact form.
