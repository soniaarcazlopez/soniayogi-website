Good to know

Online yoga, your questions

How do online classes work?

Classes are streamed live, so you practise in real time with Sonia and the group. Book through Eventbrite and you will receive a link to join. Please keep your camera on so Sonia can give you alignment cues and keep you safe.

Can I join from outside the UK?

Yes. You can join from anywhere in the world. All class times are in UK time, so please check the time difference where you are.

What do I need for an online class?

A yoga mat, a stable internet connection, and enough clear space to move safely. A cushion, blanket or blocks are helpful for Yin and meditation but not essential.

Are online classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. All classes are for all levels, with modifications offered throughout. Live classes mean Sonia can see you and guide you as you practise.

How much do online classes cost?

The weekly classes and Morning Meditations are donation based, give what you feel. There are also monthly subscription and gift packages.
